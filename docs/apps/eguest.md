# eGuest — guest app

The in-stay guest companion: digital check-in, folio view, upsell
offers, service requests, and loyalty status — the guest's window
into the hotel8 services.

## What it demonstrates

- **Digital check-in** — the guest checks in from the app; PMS
  updates the reservation and room status in the same audited flow.
- **Upsell surfacing** — Hotel8:Upsell's context-appropriate offers
  appear with acceptance flowing back as a folio charge or
  reservation change.
- **Loyalty at a glance** — tier progress and point balance live
  from the loyalty engine's events.
