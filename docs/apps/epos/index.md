# ePOS — outlet staff app

The food-and-beverage point-of-sale station: table service, check
management, menu browsing, kitchen routing views, and payment capture
— the outlet staff's daily surface.

## What it demonstrates

- **Check lifecycle in the UI** — open, order, split, merge, and
  settle checks against tables and sections.
- **Kitchen display** — routed items per preparation station with
  course sequencing.
- **Payment capture** — card capture on the outlet device; the money
  movement delegates to Bank8:Payments.
