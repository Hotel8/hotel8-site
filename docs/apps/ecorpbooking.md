# eCorpBooking — corporate booking app

The corporate traveler's booking surface: negotiated rates,
corporate policy enforcement, and authorized-user booking on behalf
of the company.

## What it demonstrates

- **Negotiated rates** — corporate rate plans surface only to the
  entitled organization's users (live role resolution decides).
- **Policy booking** — corporate policy rules constrain the
  bookable inventory; out-of-policy bookings route to approval.
- **Company structures** — cost centers, travelers, and approvers
  manage through the same audited API surface.
